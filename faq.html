Frequently Asked Questions

What are your payment methods?

We accept credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship worldwide! Shipping fees may vary by region.

What is your return policy?

You can return unused items within 30 days for a full refund.

How long will it take to receive my order?

Orders typically ship within 1-2 business days. Delivery times vary based on location and shipping method selected.

Do you offer discounts on bulk orders?

Yes, we offer discounts for bulk purchases. Please contact our sales team for more information on bulk pricing.

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking number via email. You can use this number to track your order on the courier's website.

Can I change my order after it has been placed?

Once an order is placed, we cannot guarantee changes. However, please contact customer support as soon as possible, and we will try our best to assist.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes, we offer gift cards that can be used for purchases on our website. They are available in various denominations.
